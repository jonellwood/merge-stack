**Full description**

Inherited a legacy platform held together by TODOs, stale tickets, and questionable infrastructure? Time to rebuild it one merge at a time.

Merge Stack is a programming-themed puzzle game about turning tiny code fragments into production-ready systems. Tap your workstations to generate items, combine matching pieces into higher-level tools, and deliver exactly what the support queue demands.

Every ticket is a choice. Use an item now for a reward, merge it into something more valuable, recycle it for resources, or mount it in the Server Rack until you need it. As the platform grows, so does the challenge of keeping a crowded board under control.

FEATURES

• Merge code, bugs, infrastructure, HTML, and limited-event item chains
• Resolve support tickets to earn credits, experience, and energy
• Unlock specialized generators as your engineering career advances
• Expand the board and organize valuable items in a working Server Rack
• Discover achievements and limited event badges
• Use smart merge hints and ticket-ready indicators when you need direction
• Play at your own pace with local device saves
• No ads and no pay-to-win purchases

From junior developer to legacy-platform hero, every merge brings the system a little closer to something resembling production.
