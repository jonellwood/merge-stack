MERGE STACK

Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Overview

Merge Stack is a local-first merge game developed by Jonathan Ellwood. You can play without creating an account. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, settings, achievements, save backups, and a randomly generated device identifier locally using browser storage or native app storage. This information remains on your device unless you choose to use cloud synchronization. Removing the app, clearing site data, or using browser privacy controls may delete local information.

Optional cloud accounts

The web and PWA versions offer optional sign-in and cross-device saves through Supabase. If you use this feature, we process:

  • Your email address and, if you choose Google sign-in, basic account profile information supplied by Google.
  • Your game state, including progress, inventory, settings, achievements, and in-game transaction history.
  • A randomly generated app device identifier, save revision numbers, and synchronization timestamps used to prevent save conflicts.

This information is used only to authenticate your account, synchronize and recover progress, provide game functionality, and protect save integrity. The current Android and iOS app builds store game progress locally and do not offer cloud account sign-in.

Service providers and technical data

We use Netlify to host the web app and Supabase to provide optional authentication, database storage, and synchronization. Google participates only when you choose Google sign-in. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device type, request timestamps, and security logs as necessary to deliver and protect their services. Information may be processed in countries where these providers operate.

We share information with these service providers only to operate the app. We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

Security

Network connections use HTTPS encryption. Cloud game records are protected by account-based database access controls so authenticated users can access only their own save. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains until you clear it or remove the app. Cloud account and save information remains while your account is active or as needed to operate and secure the service.

To request permanent deletion of your cloud account and associated game data, email jonellwood@gmail.com from the address connected to your Merge Stack account with the subject “Merge Stack account deletion.” We may ask you to verify ownership. Requests will be completed within 30 days unless retention is required by law or for security and fraud prevention. You may continue playing locally after cloud data is deleted.

Children

Merge Stack is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be deleted.

Your choices

  • Play without an account and keep progress only on your device.
  • Sign out at any time without deleting local progress.
  • Download a JSON backup of your game from Settings.
  • Request deletion of your cloud account and associated data using the contact above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

For privacy questions, contact Jonathan Ellwood at jonellwood@gmail.com.
